Using the product

Receipt Statement Linker helps you preserve receipt context, compare it with statement CSV rows, and export links you approve. You are responsible for reviewing every match and for the files you import or export. Do not use the product to process data you are not authorized to handle.

No financial, accounting, or tax advice

Suggestions are based on amount, date, and merchant text. They can be wrong. The product does not verify that a transaction, deduction, account category, receipt, or tax position is correct. Keep source documents and consult a qualified professional when required.

Free and paid editions

The free edition includes capture for up to 25 saved receipts, CSV matching, review, enriched CSV export, and attachment manifest export. A one-time $19 license removes the saved-receipt limit and enables library backup for the supported product version. Accessibility, export, and safety controls are never paywalled.
